Privacy Policy

Lost Route: Offroad 4x4 · Last updated: 5 November 2026

Lost Route: Offroad 4x4 ("the game") is made by an independent developer, Oguzhan Kozluca. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

We do not collect personal data

The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else. It works fully offline.

Data stored on your phone

Your game progress, settings and recorded "ghost" runs are saved only on your device, inside the game's own storage. They are removed when you delete the game. You can also erase them in the game: Garage → Settings → Reset save.

Photos

If you use photo mode, the game asks for permission to add pictures to your Photos library. It only saves the pictures you take in the game; it never reads or uploads your existing photos. Sharing a picture uses the standard iOS share sheet and only happens when you choose to share.

Notifications

The game can show a local reminder at most once a day. It is created on your phone; no data is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in the game settings or in iOS Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
