Frequently asked questions

The truck is stuck. What can I do?
Switch to 4L or Crawl, lock the differentials, lower the tire pressure, use traction boards or the winch. If nothing works, return to the last checkpoint from the hint card.

The phone gets warm.
Open Settings in the garage and pick Smoothness: 30 FPS, or a lower Graphics quality.

The night regions are too dark.
Settings → Brightness → Bright or Very bright.

Does the game need the internet?
No. Lost Route plays fully offline. Your progress is saved on your phone.
